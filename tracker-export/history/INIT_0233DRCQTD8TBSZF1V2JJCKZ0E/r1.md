## Purpose

Define GraphScript as an extension of TypeScript and JavaScript that uses function imports to form a graph and compiles to TypeScript or JavaScript.

## Scope

- Plan the language, compiler output, and graph explorer described by the user.
- Determine the boundaries and requirements through discussion.

## Success Conditions

- The intended outcome and the decisions needed to reach it are clear.

## Non-Goals

- Implement the language or tools during the initial planning effort.
